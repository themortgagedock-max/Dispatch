On the ballot Nov 3, 2026 · Amendment 3

If at least 60 percent of voters approve it, the homestead exemption from non-school taxes rises to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028. The larger amount goes to owners who are Florida residents by December 31, 2026. Later arrivals start with about $50,000 for four years. School taxes keep the $25,000 exemption. The cap on yearly assessment increases for non-homestead property would drop from 10% to 5%.

Escrow trap to avoid

Lenders often set up escrow from the seller's last tax bill. Here that's $773 a year, which would leave a buyer thousands short when the first full bill arrives in November. Ask your lender to build escrow from the post-sale estimate.

Flood insurance

A federally backed mortgage on a home in Zone AE requires flood coverage. The biggest price driver is how high the first floor sits compared to the Base Flood Elevation, which an Elevation Certificate shows.

per year, NFIP planning range

For comparison, Manatee County's average Zone AE NFIP premium is $1,614 a year (OpenFEMA, Aug 2026). That average includes many newer, elevated homes.

Two moves that can save real money

Ask for the seller's flood policy. An NFIP policy can be assigned to the buyer at closing, which can keep the existing premium instead of restarting at today's full-risk rate. And NFIP building coverage tops out at $250,000, so ask about excess flood if rebuild cost runs higher.

Wind and homeowners

On a barrier island, the wind portion drives most of the premium. A wind mitigation inspection documents features that earn credits, and a 4-point inspection is standard for older homes.

Questions

Quick answers

What will the property taxes be at 760 Longboat Ct after a sale?

At the $840,000 list price, a buyer who makes it a Florida homestead should plan on about $10,000 a year in ad valorem taxes, or about $10,770 with a non-ad valorem allowance, using 2025 adopted millage of 14.8362 and an assessed value of 85 percent of price. A second home or investment owner should plan on about $11,370. The seller's 2025 bill of $773 does not carry over.

Is 760 Longboat Ct in a flood zone?

Yes. Public records place it in FEMA Zone AE, a high-risk Special Flood Hazard Area, on FIRM panel 12081C0291F, effective August 10, 2021. A federally backed mortgage on the home will require flood insurance, and the premium depends mostly on the first-floor height shown on an Elevation Certificate.

How much will flood and wind insurance cost?

Plan for a wide range until quotes come in. For a 1920 frame home on a barrier island, wind and homeowners coverage often runs about $5,600 to $10,200 a year before mitigation credits, and NFIP flood coverage commonly runs $3,500 to $12,000 a year depending on elevation. These are planning ranges, not quotes.

How does Florida's Amendment 3 affect a buyer here?

Amendment 3 is on the November 3, 2026 ballot and needs 60 percent of the vote. If it passes, the homestead exemption from non-school taxes rises to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028 for owners who are Florida residents by December 31, 2026. People who become Florida residents later would start with a smaller exemption for four years. School taxes keep the $25,000 exemption.

Is a jumbo loan needed to buy this home?

Usually not. The 2026 conforming loan limit in Manatee County is $832,750 for a one-unit home, so at the $840,000 list price any down payment of $7,250 or more keeps the loan within conforming limits. Eli Sanderlin can price conventional, VA, jumbo, and portfolio options side by side.

Tax sources and method

  • Millage: Manatee County Property Appraiser, 2025 Final Adopted Millage Rates; Town of Longboat Key FY2026 to FY2027 budget actions as reported by the Longboat Observer.

How the insurance ranges are built

  • Flood zone, FIRM panel, and map date: FEMA flood map data in the county public record.
