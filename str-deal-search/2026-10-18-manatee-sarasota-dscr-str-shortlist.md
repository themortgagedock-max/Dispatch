## 2. Rules that reshape the map

- **Unincorporated Sarasota County** (Siesta Key houses, Gulf Gate, Nokomis, Osprey, Englewood, South Venice) **prohibits rentals under 30 days in single-family zoning.** The rule predates 2011, so state preemption doesn't override it. These areas only work for **30-day+ seasonal** rentals.
- **City of Sarasota:** 1-week minimum, annual registration, and an annual inspection.
- **Manatee County (unincorporated West Bradenton, Cortez, 34209/34210):** nightly rentals are legal today. **A county STR ordinance is being drafted** (registration, inspections, about $600/yr proposed).
- **City of Bradenton:** certificate required (Ord. 3093).
- **Longboat Key:** 30-day minimum in residential zones.
- **Holmes Beach:** 30-day minimum in R-1, 7 nights in R-2 and up.
- **Islands under $700k = condos and villas only**, with HOAs of $945–$1,600/mo and rental caps. That HOA cost kills the DSCR. Excluded.
- **Guest taxes are 13% in both counties.** Manatee has no platform collection agreement, so the owner remits the tourist tax.

## 5. Cost assumptions (what's in the math)

**DSCR test:** gross rent ÷ PITIA, with no operating costs. Many lenders haircut STR gross by about 20% or use T12 net of management, so **plan on the "@80%" column.**

**PITIA inputs:**
- Taxes reset to the purchase price: about 1.5% in Manatee, 1.35% in Sarasota, including non-ad valorem charges.
- Insurance: $7,000/yr (rental dwelling + wind + $1M STR liability).
- Flood: $1,000 in zone X, $4,500 in AE.
- Citizens requires flood coverage on all wind policies by 1/1/2027.

**Real cash flow, STR profile:**

| Line item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Full-service management | 20% (local range 18–30%) |
| Platform fees | 10% blended (Airbnb's host-only fee is now 15.5%) |
| Cleaning | 10% (offset by guest-paid fees if they're in the gross) |
| Maintenance/supplies | 8% |
| CapEx reserve | 5% |
| Utilities | $6k/yr |
| Pool/lawn/pest | $3k/yr |

**Seasonal (30-day+) profile:** management 12%, platform 2%, cleaning 3%, maintenance 6%, CapEx 5%, plus the same fixed costs.

**Reality check:** at about 7.875%, even the #1 pick is **roughly breakeven if she self-manages or uses a 10–15% co-host** (about −$7k to +$3k/yr depending on how the seller reports gross). It runs **about −$24k/yr with full-service management.** The return comes from equity build, appreciation, personal use, and possibly taxes: cost segregation plus 100% bonus depreciation, with the STR material-participation rules (average stay of 7 days or less; CPA to confirm). If she won't self-manage, push for an interest-only option or a seller-paid rate buydown.

### The screen
PropertyRadar has no rental income, so each house gets a required-rent test.
- **Required gross** = PITIA × 12 × target DSCR ÷ 0.80 (the lender haircut).
- PITIA uses list price, 25% down (7.25%), county tax, $7k insurance and flood by zone.
- Required gross is compared with an editable **benchmark gross** for each market. The benchmarks are anchored to the seller-stated incomes already in this workbook.
